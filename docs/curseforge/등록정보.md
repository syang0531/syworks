## Description (본문 — 영문, 마크다운/에디터에 붙여넣기)

**Yame** (Yet Another Minecraft Expansion) adds two connected progression axes to **Minecraft 26.2 / NeoForge 26.2**: a deep **metallurgy** tree and a **rune-based magic** system — all built from vanilla materials, no config required.

### ⚙️ Metals & Alloys
- **11 new pure metals** extracted from ordinary world blocks — tin, zinc, nickel, aluminum, silver, chromium, titanium, cobalt, tungsten, sulfur, and platinum (plus vanilla copper/iron/gold reused).
- **12 alloys** — bronze, brass, constantan, duralumin, steel, stainless steel, titanium alloy, tungsten steel, cobalt steel, electrum, and the endgame **tungsten carbide** and **platinum superalloy**.
- Each alloy comes as a **full equipment set** — 5 tools (sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe) + 4 armor pieces (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots), each with its own tier and stats.
- **Fire-immune** endgame gear: tungsten carbide, platinum, and platinum superalloy items survive lava and fire.

### 🔥 Machines
- **Extraction Furnace** — feed it common natural blocks (granite, diorite, clay, ancient debris…) plus fuel to pull out pure metal ingots. Hopper-automatable.
- **Alloy Furnace** — combine 2–3 pure metals (order-independent) to smelt alloy ingots.
- Both are craftable in survival and behave like vanilla furnaces (progress bar, lit state, fire crackle, particles).

### ✨ Rune Magic
- Craft **Runes** at the new **Rune Altar** (anvil-style: place two inputs, pull the result — no fuel, no waiting).
- Turn runes into books for **all 42 vanilla enchantments**, level-ups included — a rune-driven alternative to grinding the enchanting table.
- Theme-appropriate material for every enchantment (e.g. Sharpness from Amethyst, Mending from a Golden Apple).

### 🪄 Casting Staves & Spells
- **18 casting staves** — 6 vanilla materials + all 12 alloys — each aligned to one of **6 elements** (Fire, Frost, Storm, Holy, Nature, Shadow) with its own spell power, cooldown reduction, and affinity bonus.
- **10 active spells** — Firebolt, Frost Arrow, Lightning, Blizzard, Heal, Regeneration, Haste, Barrier, Poison Cloud, Curse — cast with **right-click**, limited only by cooldown (no mana). **Firebolt is free on every staff** so you can cast from the very start.
- Bind spellbooks to staves at the **anvil**, switch the active spell with **Shift + scroll**, track cooldowns on the HUD. Reset a staff at the grindstone.
- **3 staff-exclusive enchantments** — Spell Power (+damage/healing), Alacrity (−cooldown), and Arcane Reach (+area/projectile speed).

### 🧭 Progression
`ore block → (Extraction Furnace) → pure metal → (Alloy Furnace) → alloy ingot → (crafting) → tools & armor`, climbing `iron → steel → tungsten steel → tungsten carbide` and `ancient debris → platinum → platinum superalloy` (the capstone), while the magic tree runs in parallel from the Rune Altar.

### Requirements
- **Minecraft 26.2** (Java 25)
- **NeoForge** (see the file's listed version)
- No other mods required.

## Description (본문 — 한글, 추가 언어용)

**Yame**(Yet Another Minecraft Expansion)는 **마인크래프트 26.2 / NeoForge 26.2**에 서로 연결된 두 축 — 깊이 있는 **금속 가공** 트리와 **룬 기반 마법** 시스템 — 을 추가합니다. 전부 바닐라 재료로 동작하며 설정이 필요 없습니다.

**⚙️ 금속 & 합금** — 바닐라 블록에서 추출하는 순수 금속 11종(주석·아연·니켈·알루미늄·은·크롬·티타늄·코발트·텅스텐·황·백금) + 합금 12종(청동·황동·양은·두랄루민·강철·스테인리스강·티타늄합금·텅스텐강·코발트강·일렉트럼·초경합금·백금 초합금). 합금마다 도구 5종 + 방어구 4종 풀세트. 엔드게임 장비(초경합금·백금·백금초합금)는 **화염 면역**.

**🔥 기계** — **추출로**(자연 블록 → 순수 금속, 호퍼 자동화) + **합금로**(순수 금속 2~3종 → 합금, 순서 무관). 둘 다 서바이벌 제작 가능, 바닐라 화로처럼 작동.

**✨ 룬 마법** — **룬 제단**에서 룬 제작(모루식 즉시 방식) → **바닐라 인챈트 42종 전부**를 마법책으로 제작(레벨업 포함).

**🪄 지팡이 & 주문** — 재료별 지팡이 18종(원소 6종) + 능동 주문 10종. 우클릭 시전, 마나 없이 쿨다운만. **파이어볼트는 모든 지팡이 기본 무료.** 모루로 주문 부여, Shift+휠로 전환, HUD 표시. **지팡이 전용 인챈트 3종**(마력·신속 시전·마력 확산).

### 요구 사항
- 마인크래프트 26.2 / NeoForge 26.2 (Java 25) / 다른 모드 불필요.

## 등록 후 남은 작업 (자동 배포 연동)
1. CurseForge 프로젝트 생성 후 **숫자 프로젝트 ID**를 [`gradle.properties`](../../gradle.properties)의 `curseforge_project_id`에 기입.
2. CurseForge **API 토큰** 발급(계정 → API Tokens) 후 GitHub 저장소 **Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions**에 `CURSEFORGE_TOKEN` 이름으로 등록.
3. 릴리스: `git tag v0.1.0 && git push origin v0.1.0` → `.github/workflows/release.yml`이 빌드·CurseForge 업로드·GitHub Release 생성.
